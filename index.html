SSC / 10th Pass

SSC / 10th Pass

People with SSC or 10th pass can apply for entry-level government jobs such as:

  • Railway Group D
  • Police Constable
  • Multi Tasking Staff
  • Lower Division Clerk
  • State-level support jobs
Degree

Degree / Graduation

Graduates can apply for many central and state government jobs like:

  • SSC CGL
  • Bank Clerk / PO
  • State PSC
  • Tax Assistant
  • Office and admin jobs
B.Tech

B.Tech / Engineering

B.Tech graduates are eligible for technical and officer-level government jobs:

  • Indian Engineering Services
  • Public Sector Units
  • Technical Officer Jobs
  • Railway Technician Roles
  • Engineering department posts
PG

Post Graduation

Postgraduates are preferred for advanced and specialist government jobs:

  • UPSC Civil Services
  • Lecturer / Professor
  • Research Jobs
  • Specialist Government Roles
  • Higher administrative posts

Age Limit and Exam Pattern

SSC / 10th Pass

Age limit: Usually 18 to 30 years, depending on the post.

Exam pattern: Mostly objective-type questions, with subjects like General Intelligence, Quantitative Aptitude, English, and General Awareness.

Degree

Age limit: Usually 18 to 32 years for many exams such as SSC CGL, IBPS, and state jobs.

Exam pattern: Tier 1 objective papers, followed by Tier 2 or interview/skill test depending on the recruitment.

B.Tech

Age limit: Generally 21 to 30 years, but it varies by department and exam.

Exam pattern: Usually written exam, technical paper, personality test, and sometimes interview for engineering posts.

PG / Master Degree

Age limit: Commonly 21 to 35 years, depending on the job and organization.

Exam pattern: Competitive exam with general studies, subject knowledge, aptitude, and interview or descriptive papers.
